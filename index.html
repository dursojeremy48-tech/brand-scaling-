How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the package. Starter sites are delivered in as little as 1 day. Standard sites take 3–5 days. Premium sites with advanced features take 7–10 days.

Do I own my website after it's built?

Yes — 100%. Once final payment is made, you own every file, every line of code, and the domain. No strings attached.

Are there any hidden fees?

Never. Our pricing is fully transparent. The one-time build fee is exactly what you pay to get your site live. Monthly retainers are optional and clearly priced. No surprises.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. We handle everything technical. You just tell us what you want and we build it.

What if I want changes after the site is live?

Minor updates are included in monthly retainer packages. One-off changes outside a retainer are billed at a flat rate agreed upon before work begins.
